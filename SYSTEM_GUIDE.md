# 📚 Assessment & Roadmap System Guide

## Your Questions Answered

### 1. Is the roadmap based on assessment level?
**YES!** The system creates a **SkillProfile** from your assessment that includes:
- Skill level (absolute_beginner, beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- Strengths & weaknesses
- Learning pace
- Confidence score

The AI uses this to generate a **personalized roadmap** tailored to YOUR level.

### 2. Does it show level after finishing assignments?
**Currently:** Level badge shows after the initial **assessment** only.
**What I'm adding:** Level display after each **assignment completion** + achievement badges!

---

## How It Works

**Assessment Flow:**
```
Take Assessment → Get Skill Level → Generate Custom Roadmap → Complete Assignments
```

The roadmap difficulty, pace, and content all adjust based on your assessed skill level.

---

## Skill Levels

| Level | Score Range | Roadmap Style |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Absolute Beginner | 0-25% | Foundational topics, slow pace |
| Beginner | 26-50% | Basic concepts, moderate pace |
| Intermediate | 51-75% | Complex topics, faster pace |
| Advanced | 76-100% | Advanced projects, quick pace |
